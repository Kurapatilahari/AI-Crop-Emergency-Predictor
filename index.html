      How it works

      This student project follows the abstract’s three input groups: weather, soil and crop-growth condition. The app checks for warning signs and translates them into Green (safe), Amber (watch closely) or Red (act immediately).

      1. Collect: enter temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind, soil moisture, pH, salinity and vegetation health (NDVI).
      2. Analyze: simple rule-based checks estimate risk and identify possible stress types.
      3. Respond: read practical suggestions such as irrigation, drainage or field inspection.
      4. Learn from feedback: record what actually happened and export records as CSV for later model development.

      Important limitation: this working prototype does not connect to live weather APIs, physical sensors, satellite feeds, SMS or a trained LSTM/Random Forest/XGBoost model. It is an educational decision-support demo, not a substitute for agronomist advice. Recommendations depend on entered values and should be verified locally.
